Who we are

About Biblical Christian

Rooted in Scripture. Grounded in truth.

Biblical Christian is an independent, non-commercial educational library created to help readers understand the Christian faith through careful study of the Bible, engagement with church history, and thoughtful consideration of important questions.

Our purpose is not to replace the local church, the ministry of pastors, or personal study of Scripture. We hope to serve readers by making clear, substantial and accessible resources available to anyone who wants to examine what Christians believe and why.

Our approach

  • Scripture governs our teaching. We seek to interpret passages in context and compare Scripture with Scripture.
  • Christ is central. The person and saving work of Jesus Christ stand at the centre of the Christian faith.
  • Grace is the ground of salvation. Sinners are saved by God's grace through faith in Christ, not by the merit of their works. Good works are the fruit of salvation.
  • History matters. We learn from the faithful witness of Christians across the centuries while testing every tradition by Scripture.
  • Truth should be spoken honestly. We aim to represent other positions fairly, distinguish fact from interpretation, and correct errors when they are found.

Statement of Faith

1. The Scriptures

We believe the sixty-six books of the Old and New Testaments are God-breathed, truthful, trustworthy and the final authority for Christian faith and life. Scripture is sufficient to make us wise for salvation through faith in Christ and to equip God's people for every good work (2 Timothy 3:16–17; 2 Peter 1:20–21).

2. The one true God

We believe there is one living and true God, eternal and unchanging, who exists as Father, Son and Holy Spirit. These three are distinct persons, fully and equally God, sharing one divine essence (Deuteronomy 6:4; Matthew 28:19; 2 Corinthians 13:14).

3. Jesus Christ

We believe Jesus Christ is the eternal Son of God, truly God and truly man. He was conceived by the Holy Spirit, born of the virgin Mary, lived without sin, died as the substitute for sinners, rose bodily from the dead, ascended to the Father's right hand, and will return in glory (John 1:1–14; 1 Corinthians 15:3–8; Hebrews 1:1–4).

4. The Holy Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit is fully God. He gives new life, unites believers to Christ, indwells and sanctifies God's people, assures them of adoption, and empowers them for holy living and witness (John 3:5–8; Romans 8:9–16; Galatians 5:16–25).

5. Humanity and sin

We believe God created humanity in his image, male and female, with dignity and responsibility before him. Through Adam's fall, sin and death entered the human race. All people are sinners by nature and choice and are unable to reconcile themselves to God (Genesis 1:26–27; Romans 3:9–23; 5:12).

6. Salvation

We believe salvation is by grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone, according to Scripture alone, for the glory of God alone. Christ's once-for-all sacrifice fully satisfies divine justice for his people. God justifies the ungodly through faith, not on the basis of works. The faith that receives Christ is accompanied by repentance and produces a changed life; obedience is the fruit, never the price, of salvation (Romans 3:21–28; Ephesians 2:1–10; Titus 3:3–8).

7. The church

We believe the church is the body and gathered people of Christ, made up of those who belong to him. Local churches are called to proclaim the Word, make disciples, practise loving discipline, care for one another, and observe the ordinances Christ gave (Matthew 16:18; 28:18–20; Acts 2:42–47; Ephesians 2:19–22).

8. Baptism and the Lord's Supper

We believe baptism is the ordinance Christ gave for believers, signifying union with him in his death and resurrection. We believe the Lord's Supper is the church's continuing remembrance and proclamation of Christ's death until he comes. Neither ordinance is a human work that earns justification (Matthew 28:19; Acts 2:38–42; Romans 6:3–4; 1 Corinthians 11:23–26).

9. Christian life

We believe those whom God saves are called to grow in holiness, love God and neighbour, pursue justice and mercy, forgive one another, and bear witness to the gospel. Good works do not earn salvation; they flow from the new life God gives (Ephesians 2:10; Titus 2:11–14; James 2:14–18).

10. The return of Christ and final judgment

We believe Jesus Christ will return personally and visibly, the dead will be raised, and God will judge the world in righteousness. Those who belong to Christ will enjoy everlasting life with him, while those who remain in sin will face everlasting judgment (John 5:28–29; Acts 17:31; 1 Thessalonians 4:13–18; Revelation 21:1–4).

Our commitment

This statement summarises the convictions that guide the material published here. Individual articles may address disputed questions in greater detail. We welcome careful examination, correction from Scripture, and respectful discussion. The Bible itself—not this website—is the final authority.

Biblical Christian is an educational resource and is not a substitute for the teaching, fellowship, pastoral care and ordinances of a faithful local church.

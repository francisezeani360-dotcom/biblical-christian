Prayer: Coming to God Through Christ

Prayer is not a way of persuading a reluctant God to care for us. It is the privilege of approaching our heavenly Father through Jesus Christ, with the help of the Holy Spirit and according to God's Word.

An invitation from God

Scripture commands believers to pray and repeatedly shows God's people calling on him. Yet prayer is more than a religious duty. Through Christ, those who were once far from God are brought near (Ephesians 2:18). The believer approaches not on the strength of personal merit, but through the mediation of Jesus, our great High Priest (Hebrews 4:14–16; 7:25).

Praying to the Father

Jesus taught his disciples to pray, “Our Father in heaven” (Matthew 6:9). Christian prayer is shaped by this relationship. God is not an impersonal force; he is the Father who knows the needs of his children. This does not make prayer casual or careless. We come with reverence, confidence and dependence.

A biblical pattern

The Lord's Prayer teaches us to honour God's name, seek his kingdom and will, depend on him for daily provision, ask forgiveness, extend forgiveness to others, and seek his keeping from temptation (Matthew 6:9–13). These priorities help correct prayers that focus only on our immediate wishes.

Scripture also teaches thanksgiving (Philippians 4:6–7), confession (1 John 1:9), intercession for others (1 Timothy 2:1–4), lament in suffering (Psalms 13; 42), and praise (Psalm 103). We should bring our whole lives honestly before God.

The Spirit helps us

We do not always know how to pray as we ought. Romans 8:26–27 teaches that the Spirit helps believers in their weakness. This is comfort, not an excuse for neglect. We learn to pray by hearing God's Word, following Christ's instruction, and continuing even when our words feel small.

Growing in prayer

Set aside regular time, pray through Scripture, give thanks specifically, confess sin honestly, and remember the needs of your church and neighbours. Do not measure prayer only by emotion or length. God hears his people because of Christ, not because they have found perfect words.

Prayer does not purchase God's favour. It is one of the gifts of life with him. Come to the Father through the Son, trusting his wisdom and goodness.

Read further

Contact & feedback

Careful study welcomes honest questions, thoughtful discussion and correction when we get something wrong.

Biblical Christian is an independent educational library. If you have a question about an article, notice an error, or want to suggest a subject for a future study, you are welcome to get in touch by email.

When reporting a correction, include the page address, explain what needs attention, and provide a source where possible. For theological disagreements, explain the relevant biblical passage and your reasoning so the matter can be considered carefully.

Report a technical problem

For broken pages, display problems or other website issues, you can also use the project's public issue tracker.

Report a website issue

What feedback is useful?

  • Incorrect historical details, quotations or references.
  • Broken pages, links or accessibility problems.
  • Questions about an article's explanation of a biblical passage.
  • Suggestions for subjects that would benefit from a careful, Scripture-centred study.

Feedback is considered carefully, but submission does not guarantee that an article will be changed. The aim is accuracy, clarity and faithfulness to Scripture—not agreement for its own sake.

Contact & feedback

Careful study welcomes honest questions, thoughtful discussion and correction when we get something wrong.

Biblical Christian is an independent educational library. If you notice a factual error, a broken link, a Scripture reference that needs attention, or have an idea for a future study, you can send feedback through the project's public GitHub issue tracker.

Send feedback or report a correction

Please include the page address, explain what needs attention, and provide a source where possible. For theological disagreements, explain the relevant biblical passage and your reasoning so the matter can be considered carefully.

Open feedback form

What feedback is useful?

  • Incorrect historical details, quotations or references.
  • Broken pages, links or accessibility problems.
  • Questions about an article's explanation of a biblical passage.
  • Suggestions for subjects that would benefit from a careful, Scripture-centred study.

Feedback is considered carefully, but submission does not guarantee that an article will be changed. The aim is accuracy, clarity and faithfulness to Scripture—not agreement for its own sake.

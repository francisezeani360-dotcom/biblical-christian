Recommended study habits

  1. Begin with prayer, asking God for understanding and a willing heart.
  2. Read the passage repeatedly and in its wider context.
  3. Compare related passages rather than building a doctrine on an isolated verse.
  4. Use reliable commentaries and historical sources as helps, not as authorities above Scripture.
  5. Discuss what you learn with a faithful local church and put the truth into practice.

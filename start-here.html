A guided reading path

Not sure where to begin?

The Christian faith is centred on the living God and the good news of what he has done through Jesus Christ. This page offers a place to begin, with readings arranged to help you move from the foundations of the faith to the gospel, salvation, and the life God calls his people to live.

You do not need to read everything at once. Take your time, open the Bible, and examine each subject carefully.

These readings are arranged in a suggested order, not as a course you must complete before you can come to Christ. If you are asking how to be saved, you can go straight to the good news in Step 2. If you are already a Christian, use the path to revisit the foundations and deepen your understanding.

Each article includes Scripture references. Read the passages in their context, compare what you read with the Bible, and do not feel that you must accept a claim simply because it appears on this website. Scripture itself is the final authority.

01
Begin with the foundations

Who is God, and what is truth?

Before considering what Christianity teaches about salvation, begin with the God who made us and the Word through which he has made himself known.

Does God Exist?

Consider why belief in the Creator is reasonable and how creation bears witness to him.

The Authority of Scripture

Why the Bible is God's Word and our final authority for faith and practice.

The Trinity: One God in Three Persons

What Scripture teaches about the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit.

02
Understand the good news

Who is Jesus, and what has he done?

Christianity is not first a programme of self-improvement. It is the announcement of what God has done in his Son for sinners. These readings introduce Jesus and the heart of the gospel.

Who Is Jesus Christ? His Person and Work

Explore his identity, incarnation, obedience, death, resurrection, and reign.

The Atonement: The Work of Christ

Understand the meaning and accomplishment of Jesus' death on the cross.

Evidence for the Resurrection

Examine the historical testimony at the centre of the apostolic message.

You Need Jesus: The Good News That Can Save You

A direct explanation of sin, grace, Christ, and the call to repent and believe.

03
Explore salvation

How does God save sinners?

The Bible describes salvation through several connected truths. These articles explain God's work in giving new life, the sinner's response of repentance and faith, and the righteousness believers have in Christ.

The New Birth: Understanding Regeneration

Why we need spiritual life and how the Holy Spirit brings new birth.

What Is Saving Faith?

What it means to receive and rest upon Jesus Christ alone.

What Is Repentance?

What it means to turn from sin to God and how repentance belongs with faith.

Justification: How We Are Saved

How God declares sinners righteous through faith in Jesus Christ.

How Can I Know I Am Truly Saved?

Consider Christ's promises and the evidences of genuine faith.

04
Grow in the Christian life

What does it mean to follow Christ?

Those who belong to Christ are called to grow in holiness, depend on God in prayer, and learn to understand and obey his Word.

Sanctification: Growing in Holiness

How believers grow in obedience while depending on the Spirit's work.

Prayer Through Christ

Why Christians can approach God as Father through Jesus.

How to Study the Bible

Practical guidance for reading Scripture carefully and in context.

The Church: Its Nature, Purpose, Leadership and Mission

Why Christ gathers believers into a people who worship, serve, and bear witness.

05
Go deeper

Explore the wider teaching of Scripture

Once you have worked through the foundations, continue exploring questions about God's rule, Christian history, difficult doctrines, and the reasons for the hope we have in Christ.

The Sovereignty of God

Study God's rule over creation, providence, salvation, and history.

What Happens When We Die?

What the Bible teaches about death, resurrection, judgment, and the new creation.

The Early Church and the Council of Nicaea

Learn how early Christians defended the biblical teaching about Christ.

Has the Bible Been Corrupted?

Examine the manuscripts, textual variants, and preservation of Scripture.

What the Bible Says About Tongues

Consider the New Testament teaching on tongues and spiritual gifts.

A word about the order: These topics are connected, but the order is only a guide. You do not earn God's favour by completing a reading list or mastering doctrine. Salvation is God's gracious work, accomplished by Christ and received through faith. Read with humility, ask honest questions, and keep returning to Scripture.
